## Fresh-agent route

If you are a new agent starting from `Devaxonic-WMS`, you should be routed here for active WMS Outbound work.

Within this repository, an English-speaking agent normally follows:

1. `AGENTS.md`
2. `STATE.md`
3. `02_CANON/AUTHORITY.md`
4. `02_CANON/SOURCE_REGISTRY.md`
5. `02_CANON/OUTBOUND_GOLDEN_RECORD.md`
6. the source-specific English Architect mirror in `01_ARCHITECT_TRANSLATIONS/2026-08-31/`
7. `03_TRACEABILITY/`
8. `04_CURRENT_STATE/`
9. `07_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN/`
10. `05_EVIDENCE/EVIDENCE_STANDARD.md`

The immutable Polish originals are under `01_ARCHITECT_SOURCE/2026-08-31/`. They are ultimate provenance, but they are not a second mandatory read for normal English-agent work. Open the PL original only for exact provenance/wording, suspected translation ambiguity or defect, an apparent EN/canon inconsistency, or explicit translation validation.

If EN and PL differ semantically, treat the difference as a **TRANSLATION DEFECT**, not an architecture conflict: PL wins and EN must be corrected. The English mirror is a readable representation of the Architect Source, not an independent product-decision authority.

The implementation plan is a delivery route for approved requirements. It may not create, broaden or override business requirements.

Do not start from chat history, old Inbound waves, old owner overrides or legacy Outbound DB schemas.
